========================================================================
  KEY FINDINGS
========================================================================

  1. BEST MODEL:
     RF + diffprivlib Gaussian (84-88% accuracy at e=0.5-1.0)
     --> Uses tree-level DP, much more efficient than feature-level noise

  2. MECHANISM COMPARISON:
     For RF: Gaussian (diffprivlib) >> Laplace (feature-level)
     For LR: Laplace (feature) > Gaussian (feature) at same epsilon

  3. PRIVACY-ACCURACY TRADEOFF:
     All models: Higher epsilon -> lower privacy, higher accuracy
     RF-Gaussian: Tradeoff is gradual (15.44% -> 11.53% drop)
     LR-Gaussian: Still huge drop even at e=1.0 (64.64% drop)

  4. DP GUARANTEE STRENGTH:
     Gaussian: (epsilon, delta)-DP  -- approximate DP
     Laplace:  pure epsilon-DP      -- exact DP (stronger claim)
     Laplace gives stronger formal guarantee but lower utility here.

  5. MODEL SUITABILITY:
     Random Forest handles DP noise much better than Logistic Regression
     Health risk data is non-linear -> RF is better suited

  6. RESEARCH CONCLUSION:
     "RF + diffprivlib Gaussian achieves 84.47% accuracy with
      (0.5, 1e-5)-DP -- strong privacy guarantee for healthcare.
      Feature-level noise is insufficient for high-privacy regimes."
