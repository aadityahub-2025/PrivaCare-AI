  Key Takeaways:
    1. Baseline (No DP)    : 99.92%
    2. Best DP result      : 47.38% at e=1.0
    3. Laplace b=2.0 at e=0.5 -> noise too large for RF splits
    4. Pure epsilon-DP     : stronger formal guarantee (no delta)
    5. Accuracy lower than RF-Gaussian (diffprivlib uses tree-level DP)

  WHY ACCURACY IS LOW:
    Feature-level Laplace noise (b=2.0 at e=0.5) is too large
    relative to [0,1] feature range. RF split points become random.
    diffprivlib RF (rf_gaussian.py) applies DP at tree level -- more
    efficient use of privacy budget.
