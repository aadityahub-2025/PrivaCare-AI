  WHY ACCURACY IS LOW:
    Logistic Regression uses a linear decision boundary.
    Health data (Normal/Mild/Moderate/High Risk) is non-linear.
    Even without DP, LR baseline (98.08%) < RF baseline (99.92%).
    With Gaussian sigma=7.03 on [0,1] features, all structure is lost.
